# Loop Crystals

<ItemImage id="ae2lpt:loop_crystal" scale="4" />
<RecipeFor id="ae2lpt:loop_crystal" />

One Loop Crystal breaks into four fragments. Combine four fragments to recover a crystal, or use Certus Quartz to turn four fragments into four Loop Crystals.

<RecipeFor id="ae2lpt:loop_crystal_block" />

Arrange four crystals in a 2×2 square to make a storage block. Process one crystal in an Inscriber to make powder. The crystals and their powder are used for storage cores, factory equipment and Loop Cards.
