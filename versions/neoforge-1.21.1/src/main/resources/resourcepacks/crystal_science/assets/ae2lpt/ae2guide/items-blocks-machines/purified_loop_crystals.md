# Purified Loop Crystals

<ItemImage id="ae2lpt:purified_loop_crystal" scale="4" />
<RecipeFor id="ae2lpt:loop_crystal_seed" />

Grow Loop Crystal Seeds in water or in a Crystal Growth Chamber. Mature seeds turn into Purified Loop Crystals. Use the purified and ordinary forms together in any recipe that consumes Loop Crystals.

The Crystal Aggregator can make 32 seeds from eight powder and 32 sand for 51,200 AE. The Crystal Pulverizer can turn either form of the crystal into one powder for 8,000 AE.
